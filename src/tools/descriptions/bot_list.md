List the bots visible to you.

Visibility follows the organization tree: yourself, your ancestors, your same-parent siblings, and your own subtree. Cousin branches are not visible. The calling bot's row is marked `(self)` after its id, so you can tell which identity is your own. Each row carries the bot id, name, description, and its session's current status (`idle` / `running` / `stopping` / `running_with_subagent`). Normal sessions cannot call this tool.
